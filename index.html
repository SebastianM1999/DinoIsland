    Your first expedition

    How to play

    1. Explore and prepare

      Follow the trails, gather fruit, and use the camp workbench to craft supplies. Fruit restores health.

    2. Hunt as a team

      Track dinosaurs, choose your moment, and keep an eye on your crew. Harvest fallen dinosaurs for resources.

    3. Find the boat parts

      Your mission board lists the missing parts. Search the island, repair the boat, and gather everyone to set sail.

    4. Grow stronger

      Hunts and recovered boat parts earn experience. Open Skills with K and spend your points near camp.
