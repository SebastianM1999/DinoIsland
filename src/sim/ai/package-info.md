# src/sim/ai
> Per-species behaviour "brains" for the server-side dinosaurs: when to wander, graze, chase, attack, defend, flee or rest.

## Files
- `senses.js` — dinosaur perception for species with `CONFIG.dinos[type].ai.senses` (raptor, gloom raptor, T-Rex, Dilophosaurus, Spinosaurus, and the herbivores Triceratops, Ankylosaurus, Pachycephalosaurus, Stegosaurus, Brachiosaurus): `updateSenses(sys, dt)` once per tick before the brains (noise inbox, fading awareness, forgetting, then sight checks: a view cone with a peripheral band, `lineBlocked` from eye height to chest (and head up close), each animal every `LOOK_EVERY` s staggered by id, at most `RAY_CAP` rays per tick for all, fresh hunters first); `hear` (noise events: `NOISE` radius x species `hearing`; the memory is the noise spot, not the player); awareness levels `LEVEL` with hysteresis; `perceived(d, sys, id)` (live while seen, else remembered and led by the remembered velocity), `perceive` (best player at a level), `alarm` (hurt), `share` (pack calls), `afterDowned` (reviver, next player or nobody), `searchGoal` (predicted spot, then 3-5 points growing 6-18 m, then done), `forget`. A carried torch (`carriesLight`) is seen further and quicker, most of all under a roof; Stalker and the ash rain shorten sight. Downed, dead or safe players are never perceived. Species without `ai.senses` get live positions from `perceived`. All of it is transient (not saved). New random draws use `world.aiRng`.
- `sarcosuchus.js` — `sarcosuchusBrain`: one Alpha Sarcosuchus in island 2's central Drowned Hollow; submerged warnings, committed lunges with miss recovery, bite/shove pressure, swept tail hits, bounded pivots and lateral steps, water retreats and low-health bite/pivot/tail chains. Exports `SARCO_TIMING`, `sarcoMouthHit`, `sarcoTailSweep` for clip synchronization and combat tests; `sarcoGroundHeight` resolves absolute water support after movement so bank height is not added twice (the shared maths lives in `aquatic.js`). Emergence stays low for the full warning and rises smoothly with the committed surge; model length/height scales affect reach and immersion without shrinking width.
- `aquatic.js` — helpers shared by the water ambushers (Alpha Sarcosuchus, Sump Lurker): `toYaw`, `phase`, `submergeToward`/`flagSubmerged`, `aquaticGroundHeight`, `carcassSurface`, `mouthCapsuleHit`. Species opt in with `CONFIG.dinos[type].aquatic` (`maxWaterDepth`, `submerge {shallow, range}`, `carcassDepth`, `phaseClips`); `DinoSystem` reads the same flag for swimming and floating carcasses.
- `sumpLurker.js` — `sumpLurkerBrain`: the cave ambusher of flooded tunnels. Lies submerged (`DS.SUBMERGED`), creeps through its own water toward prey, lunges (0.6 s wind-up, 0.7 s strike, `LURKER_TIMING` synced to `SumpLurker_Attack`) at players who swim or stand within `edge` m of deep water inside `lungeRange`, drags back to the water afterwards and retreats when hurt. Leashed to its spawn pool (`d.leash`), no boss rules (no phases, bar or arena), normal respawn. Spawns only at flooded `layout.caveDinoSpots` (`floodedSpot`: truthy `water`/`flooded` or deep water at the spot), count from `level.dinos['sump-lurker']`.
- `crystalPlodder.js` — `crystalPlodderBrain`: the armoured cave herbivore. Grazes in its hall (`plodderChambers`, one per hall), peaceful until hit or crowded (`angerRadius` 3 m), then bellows, turns its rump on the offender, swings the club tail (`clubSweep`/`PLODDER_TIMING` measured from `CrystalPlodder_Attack`, strong damage + knockback) or charges a short burst at attackers out of reach, and calms after `calmTime`. 600 HP.
- `territorial.js` — helpers shared by the stego and the plodder: `makeTailSweep` (swept tail hitbox from a baked clip), `rearYaw`, `grazeWander` (calm graze/wander loop).
- `utility.js` — utility decisions (docs/dino-ai-concept.md 3.3): response curves `curve` (`linear`, `quad`, `logistic`, `step`, `inv`), `score(considerations, weight)` (product with a compensation factor), `choose(d, options, ctx, spec, now, override)` (re-thinks at `ai.utility.thinkRate` Hz staggered by id, `momentum` for the current option, `minCommit` s before it may change unless overridden; options are evaluated in name order, so ties are deterministic), `rethink`. Only picks the option; the brain's mode machine still moves and hits.
- `defender.js` — the shared brain of the defensive herbivores (`defenderBrain(type, style)`, `spawnHerd`): utility options graze / watch / threaten (the herd lines up toward the threat, `DS.ROAR`) / strike (the species' committed attack, `style.start`/`style.step`) / return (back to the leash anchor) / avoid (an attacker beyond the leash: the herd moves off) / flee (only with `ai.defend.flee`). A hit alerts the whole herd (`alarm` on every member); a downed attacker is no longer perceived, so they calm down. Strike blocks: `startCharge`/`chargeStep` (head-down wind-up, committed line with small corrections, contact, leash and blocked stop it, recover) and `startStrikeClip`/`strikeClipStep` (a timed hit synced to the baked Attack clip, in front and in reach of the live player). Tuning in `CONFIG.dinos[type].ai.defend` and `ai.utility`.
- `triceratops.js` — `triceratopsBrain` (herd of `groupSize`): horn thrust and toss up close (`HORN`, synced to Triceratops_Attack), a dodgeable committed charge from further.
- `ankylosaurus.js` — `ankylosaurusBrain`: never flees; turns its rump and swings the tail club (`clubSweep`, `makeTailSweep` with a sweep taken from the README clip timings, not yet measured from the baked clip), a few steps' charge to close the gap; leash 10 m.
- `pachycephalosaurus.js` — `pachycephalosaurusBrain`: runs from two or more players or when badly hurt; a lone player gets the challenge display, then a charge that ends in the head-butt (`BUTT`, synced to Pachycephalosaurus_Attack, big knockback).
- `brachio.js` — `brachioBrain`: peaceful herds that wander and graze, flee together, show a threat display (head high, bellow) to a player they see within `ai.defend.warn`, and defend with a telegraphed rear-up stomp when a player gets close or attacks, never following past `ai.defend.leash` from where the defence began; a hit alerts the whole herd and, while it flees, the others close ranks round the injured animal; also spawns the oversized titan leashed inside the giant's pen (`layout.grove`, `shared/grove.js`).
- `stego.js` — `stegoBrain` (tail maths via `territorial.js`): territorial grazer that gets angry at intruders it has noticed (its senses), charges in bursts and whips its spiked tail (swept-arc hitbox taken from the baked Attack clip); counters hits from the front. Exports `tailSweep`, `tailRelative` (tail geometry helpers, also used by tests).
- `raptor.js` — `raptorBrain`: pack hunter (packs stored in `sys.groups`) that notices players with its senses (stops and looks while suspicious, hunts when alert), calls the pack (shared memory), chases, flanks and does hit-and-run bites, searches where an unseen target was last known and gives up when the search is over; hits frighten it briefly. A downed target: it turns on a perceived reviver or the next player, else the pack walks away from the body. Exports `updateRaptorFear` (shared with raiders), `packHunterBrain(type, species, acquire = alertTarget)` (the pack brain, reused by `gloomRaptor.js` and `testDinos.js`), `alertTarget` and `spawnPack`.
- `gloomRaptor.js` — `gloomRaptorBrain`: the cave raptor kin; same pack logic as the raptor (`packHunterBrain`), its own senses (`ai.senses`): 12 m of sight, long hearing (walking, sprinting, shots and arrows are noise events), anything within 7 m is felt, a carried torch is seen from about twice as far under the roof. Packs spawn only at `layout.caveDinoSpots` `[{x, z, radius}]`, the count comes from `level.dinos['gloom-raptor']`; on levels without cave spots nothing spawns.
- `trex.js` — `trexBrain`: rare apex predator patrolling `layout.trexPatrol`; stops and looks when suspicious, walks over to noises it hears (`investigate`: a gunshot carries ~120 m), roars at players it sees, chases while the chase pays (`chaseWorth`: chase economics from `ai.chase`, gap not closing, distance from where it began, own HP; a weak or lone target is chased longer; it searches where it lost sight), bites hard (wind-up, the bite checks the live player), then rests. A downed target: a perceived reviver, the next player or back to the patrol.
- `ptera.js` — `pteraBrain`: nests on cliffs, circles areas of the island, dives at players (`pickTarget` prefers lone, injured (HP as a fraction of max HP) or meat-carrying ones; `diveLead` aims where the target will be, capped at `LEAD_MAX` s), knocks them down, steals carried meat and flies off; does its own 3D flight (`flyTo`).
- `testDinos.js` — `TEST_BRAINS` for the test predators without their own behaviour yet (Dilophosaurus, Spinosaurus): each runs `packHunterBrain` as a pack of one, acquiring with its senses. `ringHome(sys, type)`: the home of every new island 1 species (these two and the three herbivores) on a ring round `layout.hut`, count from `level.dinos[type]`. Replace with a real brain per species when its gameplay is written.

## Entry points
- Each file exports one brain object with this optional interface, called only by `DinoSystem` in `src/sim/dinos.js` (registered in its `BRAINS` map):
  - `spawnInitial(sys)` — from `DinoSystem.spawnAll()` for each new island.
  - `respawn(sys, r)` — when a respawn entry is due.
  - `init(d, sys)` — on `DinoSystem.spawn`, sets per-animal state.
  - `onStuck(d, sys)` — after the dino system nudged a stuck animal (not implemented by ptera).
  - `onHurt(d, sys, byId, dmg, weapon)` — after damage that did not kill.
  - `update(d, sys, dt)` — every tick, unless the animal is a raider handled by `raiderStep` (`src/sim/raids.js`).
- `updateRaptorFear` is also called from `raiderStep` in `src/sim/raids.js`.
- `sys` is the `DinoSystem`; brains use its helpers (`steer`, `halt`, `turnTo`, `nearestPlayer` (touch-range checks; predators perceive through `senses.js`), `noise`, `distTo`, `canReach`, `attackPlayer`/`hitPlayer`, `inSafeZone`, `roar`, `randomWalkablePoint`, `fleePoint`, `spawn`, `groups`) and reach the world through `sys.world`.

## Rules
- Brains import only from `src/shared/` (config, protocol `DS`/`EV`/`MSG`, `rng`, `grove`, `island`, `visibility`, `torch`) and from siblings in `ai/` (`raptor.js` for the shared pack-hunter brain, `senses.js` for perception, `utility.js` for decisions, `defender.js` for the herbivores); no imports from other sim files or the client.
- Predators act on what they perceive (`senses.js perceived`), never on a player's live position while they do not see them; damage still goes through `hitPlayer`/`attackPlayer` against the live player. Downed players are never targets (docs/dino-ai-concept.md 3.2a).
- Species tuning comes from `CONFIG.dinos.<type>` (bound as `C` at the top of each file); fixed animation-derived timings (e.g. stego `TAIL_TIME`, `SWEEP`) are kept in sync with the baked clips named in the comments.
- Brains set the network-visible state `d.st` from `DS` and flag bit `d.fl & 1` for "aggressive/defending"; `towers.js` `hostile()` reads both.
- Player damage goes through `DinoSystem.hitPlayer` / `attackPlayer` (reach check past trees, knockback, then `world.hurtPlayer`); brains never set player HP directly. Respecting the base safe zone is each brain's job (`sys.inSafeZone`). Exception to keep in mind: the pteranodon takes stolen meat straight from `p.inv.loot.meat`.
- Ground movement goes through `sys.steer` / `sys.move` (collision, walkability, slopes, grove/boss-arena exclusion); only the pteranodon moves itself in the air.
- The grove titan is a "herd of one" with a `leash`; `DinoSystem.walkable` keeps it inside the pen and everyone else out. The Alpha Sarcosuchus has a `leash.kind: 'swamp'` in Drowned Hollow and may enter its deep inland ambush pockets; combat outside that arena is blocked. Attack events carry `kind`, `duration` and `phase: {clip, started, duration, seq}`; the phase uses the authoritative world clock, so snapshots and late joins seek past elapsed wind-ups. Boss snapshot rows append the four phase fields after the normal nine fields; other species retain their row shape. The submerged and enraged flags use bits 8 and 16 respectively.

## Not here
- Shared movement, collision, traps, tracks, damage, death/loot and respawn scheduling: `src/sim/dinos.js`.
- Raid behaviour (attacking the base, retreating): `src/sim/raids.js`.
- Client-side dinosaur animation, hit spheres and interpolation: `src/client/entities/dinoViews.js` and `src/client/models/dino/`.
- Species numbers (speed, health, radius, damage): `src/shared/config.js`.
