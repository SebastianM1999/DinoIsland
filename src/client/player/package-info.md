# src/client/player
> The local player on the client: predicted first-person movement, tool use and interactions (all sent as requests to the server), the first-person viewmodel, and automatic stuck detection.

## Files
- Off-hand torch (`viewmodel.js` `#updateTorch`, its own arm `armT` whose fist is welded to the torch object, independent of `this.tool`): raised while lit and the left hand is free, lowered smoothly (light dims to an ember) for bow/trap/rifle/eating/a reload; a pistol is one-handed while it is up. `actions.js` toggles it with L (`game.torchLit`, only with `inv.torch`).
- `controller.js` — `PlayerController`: client-predicted movement: WASD, sprint/stamina, jump, gravity, terrain and rock-top following, slope sliding (not at a Hollow Mountain wall: a wall node reads as a cliff, the body just stops and keeps >= 0.2 m from the wall foot), swimming in rivers/lakes (never the sea), wading slower (in a swamp bog exactly `swampSpeedMul`, `inBog`), collider collision and step-up, the cave roof's head bump (`Terrain.ceilingAt`), knockback/knockdown, creative flight, skill effects (Adrenaline, Dash), fruit buffs (`setBuffs`: Mud Walker, Second Wind, Fireproof, Quickfoot), the volcano's hot ground (`heat`: stamina drains faster, refills slower) and ash rain (`ashOutside`, set by core/game.js: stamina refills slower), grove barrier.
- Diving (`controller.js`, `CONFIG.player.dive`): hold C while swimming to dive, Space to rise; free 3D swimming along the look direction under any river, lake or flooded tunnel (never the sea), bounded by the bottom, the surface and the roof (`ceilingAt`, the sump); buoyancy lifts an idle diver; the breath meter (`breath`) drains with the head under water and refills fast above it. The torch goes out under water (relight with L once the face is out).
- `actions.js` — `PlayerActions`: pockets 1-7 in the player's order (`this.pockets`; keys, wheel and hotbar follow it; `hud.onPocketSwap` rearranges and saves; gun pockets stay empty without firearms in the pack – the demo), the inventory's `pockets` (per-tool state) and backpack `slots`, the client-side backpack check before auto-loot/gathering and tools (spear stab/throw, bow – locked, not selectable and not drawn while `inv.bow === false`, trap placement with ghost preview, fruit eat/give, pistol/rifle/MPX shots and reload), knife butchering (hold V), revive (hold E), E-interactions (pickup, harvest, gather map resources, loot drop-off, base plots/flag, boat, wardrobe, crafting box, resupply, mission board, heal teammate), auto-loot, carry weight, and the HUD parts tied to them (hotbar, prompt, hints, dino alert, compass markers).
- `viewmodel.js` — `Viewmodel`: three two-bone cartoon arms (right, left, torch; `models/arms/`, Claude-of-Duty's rig) in the outfit's sleeves and one object per held item (spear, bow with drawable string and a thick cartoon nocked arrow (bow fixed, the arrow and hand come back with the draw; arrowhead on the crosshair at full draw), trap, fruit, knife, pistol, rifle, MPX, torch), all direct children of an identity `root` under the view camera. "The tool drives, the hand follows": each frame an item gets its base pose + the layers (idle noise sway, walk bob, mouse-turn lag springs, recoil/settle/jump/land springs, sprint, tool switching, motion clips) and each hand is solved to `item matrix x grip` (`toolGrips()`: fist fitted round the spear/riser/trap bar/knife/torch, hooked fingers at the bow nock, CoD wrist targets on the guns), so hands stay welded to the grips (the shoulder leans toward targets beyond 96% of the reach so bones never stretch). Gun base rotation is SOLVED every frame (`solveBoreQuat`): the bore axis from `nodes.muzzle` along the gun's -Z passes through the camera-space point (0, 0, -`CONVERGE`) (25 m), so the barrel is nearly parallel to the view axis and the bullet to the crosshair leaves along it; only a small `cant` roll about the bore is authored per gun, and the additive layers sit on top (`vm.flat = true` drops them, for tests). The firing hands are fitted to the grip as a cylinder (`FIRING_GRIP`, `fitCylinderPose`) with the index pad fitted onto the trigger (`fitTriggerFinger`, driven by `setTrigger`); the pistol thumb is turned into the slide (`FIRING_GRIP.pistol.thumbBase`) and the rifle support fingers fold past the cylinder fit (`SUPPORT_TUCK`), so no finger shows through the slide or over the handguard (`test/fp-hands.test.js`). Guns use Claude-of-Duty's `inspect`, `draw` and `holster` clips and our own reload clips (`reloadClips.js`; `reloadTac`, `reloadEmpty`, duration = the gun's `reloadTime`, sampled at reload fraction x duration) with slide/bolt cycle, slide lock when empty, trigger, charging handle and a magazine that follows the left hand. Tool switching is a lower/swap/raise state machine (guns play holster/draw; the knife while butchering and the fruit while eating override the selected tool); stab, place and eat are `Clip`s; the spear throw keeps `spearThrow.js`'s release contract. Drawn in the renderer's separate viewmodel pass.
- `reloadClips.js` — `buildReloadClips`, `RELOAD_TUNING`, `MAG_GRIP`, `magInHand`, `MAG_FALL_G`: the two reload clips per gun (Claude-of-Duty's keys rolled the gun off the bottom of our lower hip pose and were too long for 1.25 / 1.65 s). Keys are fractions of the reload. The gun lifts toward the screen middle and yaws/cants to show its magwell (`lift`), then: tactical = the support hand pulls the old magazine out down the well (visible), carries it below the frame (`magdrop`), fetches a new one (`fetch`, enters from below), pushes it home (`magin`, orientation and position equal the seat, so no pop) and slaps it (`slap`), back to the grip by 0.95; empty = the slide/bolt is locked back, the magazine is released and falls (`parts.drop`, free fall in view space, drawn by `Viewmodel#updateParts`), the hand fetches, inserts, slaps, then the pistol slide drops on a thumb press (hand home, gun bucks) or the rifle's palm slaps the bolt catch (`boltrelease`). The rifle is pitched muzzle-up as well as yawed so its stock swings out to the lower right corner instead of filling the frame. The support shoulder anchor blends from the idle one (`GUN_DEFS.shoulderL`, the approved idle grip) to `reloadShoulderL` during the reload (`Viewmodel#poseGun`). The hand holds the magazine as a fist round a bar (`MAG_GRIP`, per-gun `spin`, `userData.magHold`); while fetching its orientation is free so the support wrist stays under 45 degrees (`test/reload-clips.test.js`). Per-gun numbers live in `RELOAD_TUNING`; `parts.magVisible` comes from explicit hidden ranges (the base clip's blend flickers between equal keys). The fraction is capped at 0.98 by `actions.js`, so the pose at 0.98 is the hip pose with the magazine seated.
- `spearThrow.js` — `SPEAR_THROW`, `spearLaunch`, `spearReleaseRotation`, `cameraPlaneScale`: one release contract (timing, origin, velocity) shared by viewmodel, gameplay and the workshop.
- MPX is hip-fire only; it uses the same cartoon arm rig, movement springs and fitted firing hand as the other guns, with a closed support-hand fist around its vertical foregrip (four knuckles stacked along the post, thumb folded inward), using the rifle-style grip target/fitting system and clearance tuning against the actual gun mesh (including a clear firing thumb and bounded trigger travel). Its tactical reload carries/stows/seats the magazine; its empty reload also racks the side charging handle with the support hand welded to it. Valid six-pocket saves append pocket 7 without reordering.
- `pockets.js` — pocket order (DOM-free): `order[pocket]` is an EQUIP index; `loadPocketOrder/savePocketOrder` (localStorage `pockets`, a validated permutation, the identity order on missing/corrupt/blocked storage), `swapPockets`, `pocketOf`, `nextPocket` (wheel, skipping tools that cannot be held). A client-side preference: `game.eq` and everything sent to the server stay EQUIP indices.
- `stuck.js` — `StuckDetector`, `STUCK`: detects no progress, endless sliding, deep water or repeated server corrections and requests `ACT.UNSTUCK` (auto with cooldown, or manual via U).
- `weapon-preview.js` — standalone weapon workshop script: viewmodel, co-op player model, tools, outfits, gun effects, inspect and torch toggle in a test scene (no server).
- `weapon-preview.html` — page (query: `?tool=<name>` initial tool, `?zoom=<fov>` narrow off-axis view camera on the hands, workshop only; `window.workshop = { vm, gfx }` for automation) that hosts `weapon-preview.js` with its controls.

## Entry points
- `src/client/core/game.js` creates `PlayerController` (`update(dt, intent)`, `look`, `teleport` on welcome/respawn/revive, `knock` on HURT events, `setMods`, `setCreative`, `barrier`, `onDash`) and `StuckDetector` (`update`, `manual`, `onCorrect`, `reset`), and registers `PlayerActions` as one of its systems (`update`, `compassMarkers`, `fullAlert`, `vm`).
- `PlayerActions` creates the `Viewmodel` and `GunEffects` (`src/client/entities/gunEffects.js`) and subscribes to EAT, BUTCHER, REVIVE, REVIVED, HURT, DINO_HIT, DEATH events.
- Projectiles in flight (arrows, thrown spears) are handed to `src/client/entities/projectiles.js`, which sends `ACT.FIRE` / `ACT.LAND`.
- `weapon-preview.html` is opened directly in the browser as a dev tool.

## Rules
- Only the player's own position is predicted; HP, inventory, loot, kills and all action outcomes are server-authoritative. Every gameplay effect leaves through `game.net.act(ACT.*)`.
- Hit tests run on the client against the animated dino hit spheres; melee and gun hits send the hit point, zone and `rt: game.renderTime` so the server can check them against the rewound pose (lag compensation).
- Movement limits (radius, step height, slopes, swim, creative) come from `CONFIG.player` and the shared collision functions, the same ones the server uses to validate (`ServerWorld.onState`); keep both sides in sync when changing them.
- Mouse sensitivity comes from `src/client/core/settings.js` (`settings.sens`, read in `PlayerController.look`).
- Butcher stroke/ring timing uses monotonic elapsed seconds, never the movement frame delta capped at 50 ms. The duration comes with the server's confirmation (rank and knife skill); the ring stays empty until then. Only a successful `EV.BUTCHER` (`done: true`) briefly holds the final stroke and full ring before recovery.
- One butchering per press of V (`knifeLatch`): after a completion, cancellation or refusal the knife waits for V to be released, so the ring never starts over by itself.
- The spear release timing/origin lives only in `spearThrow.js`; the viewmodel and gameplay must read it from there.
- The stuck detector only asks; the server decides the spot (`src/sim/unstuck.js`).

## Not here
- Key bindings and pointer lock: `src/client/input/`.
- Remote players, dinosaurs, items, projectiles in flight: `src/client/entities/`.
- Weapon, hand, fruit and firearm meshes: `src/client/models/`.
- HUD widgets and panels themselves: `src/client/ui/`.
- Validation of every action: `src/sim/world.js` (`onAct`), guns in `src/sim/firearms.js`.
