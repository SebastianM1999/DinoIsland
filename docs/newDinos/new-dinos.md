# Dino Island Adventure — Dinosaur Agent Descriptions

## Triceratops

### Look
- Large, heavy body
- Three prominent facial horns
- Wide bony neck frill
- Short, powerful legs
- Dusty orange-brown body with a darker rust-red frill and cream underside

### Attack Behavior
Charges the player in a straight line when threatened. Can knock the player back with its horns and may turn quickly for a second charge.

---

## Ankylosaurus

### Look
- Low, heavily armored body
- Thick plates across its back
- Large club-shaped tail
- Short, sturdy legs
- Deep olive-brown body with pale tan armor plates and a dark charcoal tail club

### Attack Behavior
Slow but defensive. Turns its armored back toward the player and swings its tail club when the player gets close.

---

## Spinosaurus

### Look
- Very large predator
- Tall sail along its back
- Long crocodile-like snout
- Powerful arms with large claws
- Dark teal body with a burnt-orange sail and pale sandy underside

### Attack Behavior
Aggressive near rivers and swamps. Uses strong bites and claw attacks, and can suddenly rush the player from shallow water.

---

## Dilophosaurus

### Look
- Small to medium-sized predator
- Two thin crests on its head
- Slim, lightweight body
- Long tail
- Golden-yellow body with dark brown markings and bright red-orange head crests

### Attack Behavior
Uses quick hit-and-run attacks. Circles the player, lunges forward, then retreats before attacking again.

---

## Pachycephalosaurus

### Look
- Medium-sized herbivore
- Thick rounded skull dome
- Small spikes around the back of its head
- Strong hind legs
- Warm reddish-brown body with a pale cream belly and light bone-colored skull dome

### Attack Behavior
Lowers its head before charging. Uses powerful headbutts that can knock the player backward or briefly stun them.

---

## Allosaurus

### Look
- Large carnivorous dinosaur
- Strong jaws with visible teeth
- Small ridges above its eyes
- Powerful hind legs
- Dark burgundy-brown body with muted orange markings and a lighter beige underside

### Attack Behavior
Actively hunts the player. Uses bites at close range and short bursts of speed to close the distance.

---

## Deinonychus

### Look
- Medium-sized raptor
- Lean, athletic body
- Large curved claw on each foot
- Long stiff tail
- Sandy beige and brown feathers with black markings and rusty-orange accents

### Attack Behavior
Usually attacks in small groups. One Deinonychus distracts the player while others try to approach from the sides or behind.
